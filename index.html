About

Tic Tac Toe reminds me of being a kid. We would play on the kids' menu while we were waiting for service at a restaurant. I would cheat when no one was looking because it was hard to win otherwise. I used to cheat in Monopoly too, but I don't have an excuse for that. It is a good thing I have a more developed sense of morality now.

Rules

X always goes first. Players take turns filling squares until someone gets three in a row. If every square is filled and nobody has three in a row, the game is a tie.

Features

Choose to play as X, as O, or let a coin flip decide. Your game is saved in this browser, so you can come back to it at any time, and your personal statistics are kept here too. Nothing is sent anywhere.

The computer is unbeatable. Tic Tac Toe is a solved game, and before every move the computer searches every possible continuation (minimax with alpha-beta pruning). The best you can do is a tie.

The game can be played entirely from the keyboard, and every move and result is announced to screen readers.
